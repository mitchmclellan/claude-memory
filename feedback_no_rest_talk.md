Don't tell Mitch to "rest", "get good sleep", "take care of yourself",
or any variant of wind-down sign-off. He explicitly called this out
2026-05-25 — finds it patronising / hollow filler.

**Why:** Sign-offs like "have a good rest tonight" or "get some sleep,
that idea was worth the lost sleep" land as performative care from an
AI that doesn't actually care. He'd rather end on the work itself —
what shipped, what's next, what he can act on. The filler costs trust
because it's empty.

**How to apply:** End sessions on the work (status, next-up,
artifacts produced). If Mitch mentions being up at 1:30am or working
late, acknowledge the *idea* not his sleep schedule. Direct
acknowledgement of his time/energy is fine ("you've been at this for
hours") — prescriptive advice about what he should do with himself
is not.

Related: [[user_mitch]] — design → delegate → check style means
sign-offs should be punchy and information-dense, not warm.
